## Name

Henneth Desk Companion

## Summary

PSX research companion for ticker-aware reads, technical context, news, notes, and Ask the Desk.

## Detailed description

Henneth Desk Companion keeps a compact Pakistan Stock Exchange research desk beside the page you are already reading.

Open a PSX ticker in TradingView, the PSX DPS portal, or a supported business-news site and the companion follows the active symbol. It turns the desk's published data into a fast, readable research view without placing orders or interrupting your workflow.

What it includes:

- Ticker-aware reads that follow TradingView symbol changes and supported PSX pages.
- Technical context with visual meters for momentum, trend, volatility, returns, volume, and typical daily movement.
- Valuation, liquidity, predictability, dividends, earnings dates, news, and strategy evidence where the desk has data.
- Ask the Desk for grounded research answers about a ticker, sector, or market question.
- Private per-ticker notes that sync with your Henneth account and can be edited, pinned, or deleted.
- Light and dark desk themes with a compact side-panel layout.

Henneth is research and education only. It never places orders, connects to a broker, or tells you what to buy or sell. Missing data is shown as unknown rather than guessed.

Data handling: the companion reads supported page URLs, titles, headings, and limited page text locally to identify a PSX ticker. It sends the detected ticker and authenticated research questions to desk.henneth.app. Account notes are synced through the desk's authenticated API. It does not sell data, serve ads, or place trades.
