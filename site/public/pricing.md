# Pricing — Henneth

Status: **free early access.** There is no charge, no trial clock, and no checkout. Everything the
terminal does is open to anyone with an account while this lasts.

Canonical page: https://henneth.app/plans/

## Early access

- Price: Rs 0
- Billing: none — no payment gateway is connected
- Who: anyone. Create an account at https://desk.henneth.app and you are in.
- Limits: none imposed by plan. The desk covers the companies it covers; that is the only ceiling.
- Ends: unannounced. Paid plans go live only once the published track record has resolved calls, a
  Pakistani payment gateway is connected, and the legal pages have been reviewed.

## What early access includes

- Plain-English company reads on every covered PSX name
- Model fair value, computed four ways, with the disagreement between them shown rather than
  averaged away
- Backtested strategies, runnable against the covered universe
- Screener, scanner and scenario tools
- The Desk Room analyst debates in full — bull and bear argued out in the open
- Dividends, earnings and book-closure calendars
- Practice portfolio (virtual Rs 500,000)
- Global index and sector-ETF context alongside the PSX work
- All calculators, which need no account at all: https://henneth.app/tools/

## Planned paid tiers

Not live. Listed so the direction is legible, not as an offer. Prices below are the intended
ladder and are subject to change before launch. Currency: PKR. Annual is priced at ten months, so
two months are free.

### Investor — intended Rs 3,000/month, Rs 30,000/year
For someone who wants to read a company for themselves.
Company reads, the guided investor journey, practice portfolio, dividend and earnings calendars,
all calculators. Intended 14-day trial.

### Pro — intended Rs 8,000/month, Rs 80,000/year
The full desk.
Everything in Investor, plus model fair value four ways, running any tested strategy, screener and
scenarios, ask-the-desk, alignment and portfolio X-ray, and the Desk Room debates in full.
Intended 14-day trial.

### Broker — price on request
For desks and institutions. Scope is a conversation, not a published feature list.
Contact: hello@henneth.app

## What Henneth is not

Educational research and analytics tooling. Not an investment adviser and not a broker. It gives
no personalised advice, publishes no buy/sell/hold rating, price target or stop-loss on any named
security, and places no orders. Execution is always the reader's own decision.
