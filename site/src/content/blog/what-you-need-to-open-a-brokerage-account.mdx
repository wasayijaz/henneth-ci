Every PSX brokerage account is opened on the same document — a Customer Relationship Form (CRF) —
but what has to go with it depends entirely on who's opening it. An individual, a joint pair, a
minor, a non-resident and a company each attach a different set of enclosures to the same form, and
one of those checklists changed recently: PSX, NCCPL and CDC issued a joint guideline on minor
trading accounts on 6 November 2025, replacing what used to be an ad-hoc, broker-by-broker process.
This piece breaks the checklist out by holder type, sourced to PSX's own CRF templates and that
notice directly, rather than the generic "bring your CNIC" line most guides stop at.

## One form, several annexures

The Customer Relationship Form is the account-opening document behind both a [CDC Sub Account and
an Investor Account](/blog/cdc-sub-account-vs-investor-account/) — it's the merged KYC form that
replaced the separate trading-account and CDC forms when NCCPL took on the Centralized KYC
Organization (CKO) role. PSX publishes two CRF templates: one for individuals (which covers joint
applicants and nominees within the same form) and one for a company or other body corporate. What
changes below is which enclosures attach to which template — not the underlying process.

A few lines in that table are worth pulling out, because they're the parts a generic checklist
usually flattens.

**Joint accounts share the Individual template, with one restriction.** The CRF's nomination
section — naming who inherits the account without probate — only applies to a sole individual
account. Under the nomination rule in Companies Act 2017 §79, as cited on PSX's own form, a nominee
must be a spouse, father, mother, brother, sister, son or daughter, and a joint account can't carry
one at all. The form's general terms also make joint holders liable "jointly and severally" for the
account — each holder is on the hook for the whole account, not just their notional share.

**Minor accounts are new, and specific.** Before November 2025, opening an account for a minor was
handled inconsistently across brokers. [PSX/N-1194](https://dps.psx.com.pk/download/attachment/265105-1.pdf)
standardises it: the account is titled in the minor's name with the guardian noted, the UIN is
registered under the guardian, and the guardian — not the minor — holds sole operating authority
until the child turns 18. The guideline also closes off the higher-risk order types entirely for a
minor's account: no futures contracts, no Margin Trading System or Margin Financing, no Securities
Lending and Borrowing, no Negotiated Deals Market, and no same-day buy-and-sell squaring off. At the
age of majority, the guideline sets out a defined transition: the minor's holdings move into a new
account opened in their own name (via a fresh CRF or a Sahulat Account under a new UIN), and that
transfer itself carries no capital gains tax — CGT applies only from the new account's own holding
period forward, tracked on a first-in-first-out basis.

**The cash cap is a broker reporting trigger, not just a suggestion.** PSX's Individual CRF sets
Rs 25,000 as the ceiling for cash funding; above that, the deposit has to move by crossed cheque,
bank draft, pay order or electronic transfer. A broker who accepts cash above that threshold in an
exceptional case is required to report it to PSX within one trading day.

## The simplified route: Sahulat Account

Outside the CRF entirely, PSX offers a lighter-weight option aimed at first-time, low-complexity
investors: the [Sahulat Account](https://www.psx.com.pk/psx/resources-and-tools/investors/sahulat-account).
Opening one needs only a simplified account-opening form and a copy of the CNIC — no additional
income documentation, for a customer assessed as low-risk. The trade-off is real restriction, not
just less paperwork: a Sahulat Account can only be held by a single individual, with **no joint
holder permitted at all**; it's capped at PKR 3 million per account (an investor can open one per
broker, at multiple brokers, but not two at the same broker); and trading is restricted to the
Ready Market — the Margin Trading System, Margin Financing, Securities Lending and Borrowing, and
the Negotiated Deals Market are all off-limits, the same restriction the minor's account guideline
applies separately. A Sahulat Account holder can convert to a full CRF-based trading account at any
time.

## What this doesn't cover

Two things came up in researching this piece that don't have a primary source solid enough to state
here. CDC's own fee schedule for Investor Account admission and its more detailed guidance on
biometric exceptions sit behind Cloudflare's bot protection and couldn't be read directly this
round — the [Sub Account vs Investor Account
piece](/blog/cdc-sub-account-vs-investor-account/) covers what is verifiable about that comparison.
And this piece deliberately doesn't restate the account-opening sequence itself, or the ongoing
costs of trading once the account exists — [how to start investing on the
PSX](/blog/how-to-start-investing-psx/) already covers both in full.

None of the checklist above is company-specific — the same documents open an account whether the
first position on the watchlist ends up being a bank or an exploration name on [the board](/psx/).
Once the account and CDC side are settled, sizing that first position is a separate calculation —
the desk's [position size calculator](/tools/position-size-calculator/) runs that against a stop
distance rather than a round number.
